fix(output): 1080p outputs on builds other than PPSA01341 1.07

version the HUD patch is not found and the 1080p tier lays the HUD out off screen.

  the 1080p tier and patch are used. On any other build 1080f renders the 3D at 1080 lines with the 4K output
  (KYTY_RENDER_HEIGHT=1080, unless -RenderHeight is given) and 1080q is the 4K output; an "output:" line says

  The 1.07 HUD mod (a 9-byte 0xCC cave plus three 1-byte edits) could otherwise be placed at any int3 run of
  another build. It is now TooShort there; at its own offset it applies as before.

- Tests: GamePatchLocateTests covers a padding-anchored cheat (fails on the old header) and a moved cheat with a
  padding cave; the Demon's Souls title test expects PPSA01342 supported (demonsSouls.h supports it).
